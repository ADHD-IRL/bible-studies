This **Code of Conduct** establishes a standard for communication within our group, drawing directly from our study of the scriptures.

## I. Our Commitment to Honesty

- **Total Integrity:** We commit to speaking the truth to one another at all times.
- **Rejection of Deceit:** We will not lie to one another, acknowledging that we have put off our "old self" and its dishonest practices.
- **Faithful Action:** We recognize that "lying lips" are an abomination to the Lord, and we strive to be His delight by acting faithfully.

## II. Our Commitment to Love

- **Christ-Centered Growth:** We will speak the truth specifically "in love" so that our community may grow up in every way into Christ.
- **Pure Intentions:** Our communication must always issue from a pure heart, a good conscience, and a sincere faith.
- **Joy in Righteousness:** We will not find satisfaction in wrongdoing or gossip but will instead rejoice whenever the truth is honored.

## III. Our Commitment to Peace and Freedom

- **Peaceful Resolutions:** In all our judgments and disagreements, we will seek outcomes that are both true and "make for peace."
- **Liberation through Truth:** We share the truth with the understanding that it is the tool God uses to set people free.
- **Eternal Standards:** We will anchor our conversations in the Word of God, accepting that His righteous rules endure forever.

## The Member's Pledge

"I commit to speaking the truth in love, putting away falsehood, and seeking the peace of this community through sincere and faithful speech."
