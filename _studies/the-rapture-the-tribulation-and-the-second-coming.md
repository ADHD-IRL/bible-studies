## The Rapture of the Church

- **1 Thess. 4:16:** Describes the Lord descending from heaven with a shout, the voice of an archangel, and God's trumpet. The dead in Christ will be the first to rise.
- **Titus 2:13:** Encourages believers to eagerly look for the blessed hope and the glorious appearing of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ.
- **John 14:1-3:** Jesus comforts His disciples by telling them not to let their hearts be troubled. He explains He is going to His Father's house to prepare mansions for them, and He promises to come again and receive them so they can be where He is.
- **1 Cor. 15:51-53:** Paul reveals a mystery: believers will not all "sleep" (die), but will be instantaneously changed at the last trumpet. The dead will be raised incorruptible, and mortal bodies will put on immortality.
- **1 Thess. 4:15-5:11:** Explains that living believers will not precede the dead in Christ. Once the dead rise, living believers will be "caught up together" with them in the clouds to meet the Lord. Paul then warns that the "day of the Lord" will come like a thief in the night, bringing sudden destruction to the unaware world. Believers, however, are "sons of light" and should remain sober and watchful, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, knowing God has appointed them to obtain salvation rather than wrath.
- **1 Cor. 3:11-15:** Establishes Jesus Christ as the only foundation. It warns that anyone building upon this foundation with various materials (gold, silver, wood, hay) will have their works tested by fire on the Day of Judgment. Enduring work yields a reward, while burned work results in loss, though the builder will still be saved "yet so as through fire."
- **2 Cor. 5:10:** Declares that every believer must appear before the judgment seat of Christ to receive what is due for the things done in the body, whether good or bad.

## The Tribulation Period

- **John 14:1-3:** Jesus assures His disciples of His promise to return and take them to the heavenly home He has prepared.
- **1 Thess. 4:13-18:** Paul comforts the church regarding believers who have died, explaining the sequence of the Rapture where both the resurrected dead and transformed living saints are caught up in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air.
- **Jer. 30:7:** Describes an unparalleled day of distress known as the "time of Jacob's trouble," though it promises that he will ultimately be saved out of it.
- **Dan. 9:27:** Prophesies a ruler who will confirm a covenant with many for one "week" (seven years). In the middle of this week, he will end sacrifices and bring about the abomination of desolation until the decreed consummation is poured out on him.
- **Dan. 12:1:** Foretells a time of unprecedented trouble when the great prince, Michael, stands watch. During this time, everyone whose name is written in the book will be delivered.
- **2 Thess. 2:7-12:** Warns that the "mystery of lawlessness" is already working but is being restrained. Once the Restrainer is taken out of the way, the lawless one will be revealed. Christ will destroy him with the brightness of His coming. God will send a strong delusion upon those who rejected the truth and took pleasure in unrighteousness.
- **Rev. 16:** Details the outpouring of God's seven bowls of wrath upon the earth. These severe judgments include loathsome sores on those with the mark of the beast, seas and rivers turning to blood, scorching solar heat, terrifying darkness over the beast's kingdom, the Euphrates river drying up to prepare the way for the kings from the east, demonic spirits gathering nations for the battle of Armageddon, and a final devastating earthquake accompanied by massive hail.
- **Matt. 24:27-31:** Jesus compares His return to lightning shining from the east to the west. Following cosmic signs (the sun darkening, stars falling), the sign of the Son of Man will appear. He will come on the clouds with power and great glory, sending His angels with a loud trumpet to gather His elect.
- **Matt. 25:31-46:** Depicts the judgment of the nations. The Son of Man sits on His throne and separates people as a shepherd divides sheep (the righteous) from goats (the cursed). The righteous inherit the kingdom for ministering to Christ's brethren, while the cursed are sent to everlasting punishment for ignoring their needs.
- **2 Thess. 2:7-12:** Reiterates the rise of the lawless one, his Satanic signs and lying wonders, and the strong delusion sent upon unbelievers.
- **Dan. 12:2, 3:** Prophesies a bodily resurrection where some awaken to everlasting life and others to shame and everlasting contempt. The wise will shine eternally like the stars.
- **Rev. 20:4-6:** Describes the "first resurrection," where those who were beheaded for their witness to Jesus and who refused the beast's mark come to life to reign with Christ for a thousand years.
- **Dan. 9:24-27:** Outlines the seventy weeks appointed for Israel to finish transgression and bring in everlasting righteousness, predicting the cutting off of the Messiah, the destruction of the sanctuary, and the final week of desolation.
- **Matt. 24:15-31:** Jesus warns that when the "abomination of desolation" stands in the holy place, those in Judea must flee. He predicts a "great tribulation" unequaled in history, warns against false christs, and describes His glorious return.
- **Matt. 25:31-46:** Details the final judgment of the sheep and the goats upon Christ's return.

## The Second Coming and the Millennial Reign

- **Matt. 25:31:** States that when the Son of Man comes in His glory with all the holy angels, He will sit on the throne of His glory.
- **Luke 1:32, 33:** The angel Gabriel prophesies that Jesus will be great and called the Son of the Highest. God will give Him the throne of His father David, and He will reign over the house of Jacob forever with a kingdom that has no end.
- **Acts 1:10, 11:** Following Christ's ascension, two men in white apparel assure the disciples that Jesus will return in the exact same manner as they saw Him go into heaven.
- **Acts 2:29, 30:** Peter explains that King David, as a prophet, knew God had sworn an oath to raise up the Christ to sit on David's throne.
