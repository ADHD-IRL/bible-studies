Verses about avoiding evil call believers to stay far from evil itself and from things that shape their hearts toward darkness, and that same posture should guide decisions about watching shows like *Stranger Things*.

## Key Verses About Avoiding Evil

- **1 Thessalonians 5:22:** "Abstain from all appearance/form of evil," calling Christians to reject evil in every shape it takes.
- Other passages echo this by urging believers to hate evil and cling to good, walk wisely in evil days, flee what tempts, resist the devil, and keep close company only with the wise.
- These verses together form a pattern: do not just avoid committing obvious sins, but also step away from influences, paths, and habits that pull the heart toward them.

## Applying This to Stranger Things

- *Stranger Things* clearly portrays spiritual darkness: a hell-like parallel world ("the Upside Down"), monsters that resemble demonic forces, and a story steeped in fear, psychic powers, and paranormal evil. Many Christians note that it also powerfully depicts sacrificial love, courage, and a real battle between good and evil, which can echo biblical themes of spiritual warfare and redemption.
- Because Scripture calls believers to reject every kind of evil, the question is not only "Is this show fiction?" but "What is this doing to my conscience, desires, and view of good and evil?" If it feeds fear, fascination with occult-like power, or desensitization to darkness, it clashes with the call to abstain from evil in all its forms.

## Practical Discernment Questions

- Does watching it help you hate evil more and love what is good, or does it make darkness feel entertaining and normal?
- After watching, are you more drawn to prayer, purity, and peace, or more filled with fear, intrusive images, or curiosity about dark spiritual realities?
- Would you be at peace watching it with a younger or weaker believer and saying this is a wise use of your time before God, given the command to avoid every kind of evil and to walk circumspectly because the days are evil?

## A Balanced Way Forward

- Some mature Christians, aware of these verses, still choose to watch selectively and in moderation, using the show as a starting point to talk about spiritual warfare, sacrificial friendship, and the reality of evil being confronted and defeated.
- Others, applying the same verses, conclude that the horror tone, occult motifs, and fear it stirs make it unwise for them or their families and so abstain entirely as an expression of "abstaining from every form of evil." In either case, the aim is a tender conscience, readiness to stop if the Spirit convicts, and a commitment to fill the mind with what is true, honorable, and pure.

## Key Biblical Principles

Watching a show like *Stranger Things* is an area of Christian liberty that requires careful, prayerful discernment rather than a simple universal "yes" or "no." Scripture gives principles about avoiding evil, guarding the heart, and not becoming desensitized to darkness that you can apply to this specific show.

- **Avoid what pulls you toward evil.** Believers are called to "turn... from evil and do good" and to keep their feet from evil paths, which includes what they willingly meditate on for entertainment. If the show stirs fear, fascination with the occult, or temptation (violence, impurity), that is a red flag for you personally.
- **Guard your mind and affections.** Teaching on discernment stresses that Christians should refuse entertainment that normalizes what God hates or trains the heart to laugh at sin. The question is not only "Is it fictional?" but "What is this doing to my desires, my conscience, and my view of good and evil?"

## Specific Issues with Stranger Things

- The series prominently features monsters, an "Upside Down" realm of spiritual darkness, possession-like control, and horror elements that resemble demonic forces. Some Christian commentators see valuable themes of good vs. evil, sacrificial love, and even parallels to spiritual warfare in how evil is exposed and fought.
- Others warn that its occult framing, intense fear, and graphic elements may open doors to fear, unhealthy curiosity about dark powers, or recurring disturbing images, especially for sensitive viewers or younger believers.

## Questions to Ask Before Watching

Many pastors and ministries suggest running doubtful entertainment through questions like these:

- Does this draw the heart toward God, or dull affection for God, prayer, and Scripture afterward?
- Does it feed fear, fascination with the occult, or sinful desires, or does it help you hate evil more and love what is good?
- Would you be comfortable watching it with a younger or weaker Christian and saying, "This is a good use of our minds before God"?

## Applying "Appearance of Evil"

- "Abstain from every form of evil" means staying away from what clearly embodies or celebrates what God calls darkness. If *Stranger Things* feels more like entertainment in darkness than a thoughtful portrayal of darkness being defeated, the safe and obedient choice for you may be to avoid it.
- At the same time, some mature believers watch it selectively, in moderation, and use it as a springboard to talk about spiritual warfare, sacrificial love, and the reality of evil, while guarding their hearts. The key is a tender conscience, willingness to stop if the Spirit convicts you, and a priority on filling your mind with what is true, honorable, and pure.

## Verses That Speak to Avoiding Evil

Several verses warn believers to avoid evil itself and anything that looks like or leads toward it. Here are key passages that speak directly to avoiding "every appearance" or form of evil.

### Core "Appearance of Evil" Verse

- **1 Thessalonians 5:22:** "Abstain from all appearance of evil" (KJV), often translated "abstain from every form of evil." This calls Christians to keep away from evil in any shape it takes, not just obvious, outward sins.

### Verses About Fleeing Evil

- **Romans 12:9:** Believers are told to hate what is evil and cling to what is good.
- **1 Peter 3:11:** "Turn from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it," emphasizing an active turning away.
- **Proverbs 4:27:** "Do not turn to the right or the left; remove your foot from evil," stressing careful avoidance.

### Avoiding Environments and Influences of Evil

- **1 Corinthians 15:33:** "Bad company corrupts good character," warning that people and settings associated with evil can shape a person's life.
- **Ephesians 5:11:** "Have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather expose them," urging separation from dark deeds, not just non-participation.
- **2 Corinthians 6:17:** "Come out from among them and be separate... touch no unclean thing," describing a clear line between God's people and unclean practices.

### Verses About Inner Posture Toward Evil

- **James 4:7:** "Submit yourselves... to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you," tying resistance to evil to submission to God.
- **1 John 2:15-17:** Do not love the world or the things in it; the desires opposed to God are passing away, but doing God's will lasts forever.

Together, these verses show that Scripture calls believers to stay far from evil itself, from situations that resemble it, and from influences that can pull the heart toward it.
