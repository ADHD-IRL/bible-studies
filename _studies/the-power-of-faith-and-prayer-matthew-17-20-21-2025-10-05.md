This passage records Jesus' response to His disciples after they failed to cast a demon out of a young boy (Matthew 17:14-19). It is a profound teaching on the nature and power of faith, and in some translations, the spiritual disciplines required for overcoming certain obstacles.

## 1. Scripture Reading & Context

> He replied, "Because you have so little faith. Truly I tell you, if you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you." *[But this kind does not go out except by prayer and fasting.]*
>
> Matthew 17:20-21 (NIV)

- **Context:** The disciples had been given authority to cast out demons (Matthew 10:1), yet they failed in this specific instance. Their question to Jesus, "Why could we not drive it out?" (v. 19), reveals their perplexity and prompts this teaching on faith.

## 2. Key Themes and Interpretation

### A. The Quantity of Faith (Verse 20)

- **"Because you have so little faith" / "unbelief":** Jesus directly attributes the disciples' failure to their lack of faith (or their "unbelief" in some translations). The Greek word used often implies an absence of trust in God's power, not merely a small amount of faith. They were relying on their own abilities rather than on Christ's strength and authority.
- **"Faith as small as a mustard seed":** The mustard seed was proverbial for its tiny size. Jesus is not saying the *size* of the faith is what matters, but the *nature* of the faith and the *object* in which it is placed. A small, genuine, and living faith placed in a **Great God** is what accomplishes the miraculous. Just as a mustard seed grows into a large plant, true faith, though it may start small, is living and grows.
- **"Say to this mountain, 'Move'... Nothing will be impossible for you":** "Moving mountains" is a metaphor for overcoming seemingly impossible, insurmountable obstacles or difficulties in life. This promise assures us of the limitless power of God that works through even a small amount of genuine faith, provided our request aligns with God's will and purpose. The focus is on the power of God, not the power of the person praying.

### B. The Kind of Victory (Verse 21)

- **Note on Verse 21:** Many modern biblical translations omit this verse or place it in a footnote because it is absent from the earliest and most reliable Greek manuscripts (e.g., Codex Vaticanus and Codex Sinaiticus). However, it is present in other ancient manuscripts and its parallel appears in Mark 9:29 ("This kind can come out only by prayer").
- **"This kind [of demon] does not go out except by prayer and fasting":** If included, the verse teaches that certain, more obstinate spiritual challenges require heightened spiritual discipline. Prayer and fasting are not a *substitute* for faith, but rather the *means* by which faith is strengthened, deepened, and prepared for more difficult battles. They are practices that humble the soul, increase spiritual focus, and cultivate an intense, total dependence on God. This suggests that the disciples' "little faith" (v. 20) was a result of their failure to cultivate it through deeper spiritual discipline.

## 3. Discussion and Application Questions

1. **Self-Reflection:** Jesus said the disciples failed due to "little faith." In what areas of your own life do you find yourself relying on your own strength or being crippled by "little faith" when facing a challenge?
2. **Mustard Seed Faith:** What is your understanding of the phrase "faith as small as a mustard seed?" Does it give you comfort or challenge you? How can we ensure our faith, no matter the size, is correctly focused on the "Great God" rather than our own efforts?
3. **Moving Mountains:** What are the "mountains" or seemingly impossible obstacles in your life, your community, or the world that you are asking God to move? (Examples might include addiction, unforgiveness, a broken relationship, deep-seated fear, etc.)
4. **Prayer and Fasting:** If verse 21 *is* considered, how do the spiritual disciplines of prayer and fasting work to strengthen your faith and prepare you to face spiritual resistance? How might you incorporate a greater intensity of prayer and fasting into your life for a specific spiritual "mountain"?
5. **Connecting Faith to God's Will:** How does the principle of "nothing will be impossible for you" relate to the importance of praying according to God's will (1 John 5:14)?
