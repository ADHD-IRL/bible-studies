## Core Truths of Identity

- **The Spirit of Adoption:** Believers are not meant to live in fear or slavery; instead, they have received a Spirit of adoption that allows for an intimate relationship with the Father.
- **The Witness of the Spirit:** Internal confirmation of this identity comes from the Holy Spirit bearing witness with the believer's spirit.
- **A Given Right:** Becoming a child of God is a right granted to those who receive Jesus and believe in His name.
- **Divine Initiative:** Adoption was predestined through Jesus Christ according to the purpose of God's will.
- **The Evidence of Leading:** A hallmark of being a child of God is being led by the Spirit.
