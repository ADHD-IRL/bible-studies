The word "mustard" (specifically referring to the "mustard seed") is used in the New Testament of the Bible in the Synoptic Gospels, primarily in two contexts: the Parable of the Mustard Seed and a lesson on the power of faith.

Here are all the chapters and verses where "mustard" is referenced:

| Book | Chapter:Verse(s) | Context |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| **Matthew** | **13:31-32** | The Parable of the Mustard Seed (Kingdom of Heaven compared to a mustard seed) |
| **Matthew** | **17:20** | Faith as small as a mustard seed can move mountains |
| **Mark** | **4:30-32** | The Parable of the Mustard Seed (Kingdom of God compared to a mustard seed) |
| **Luke** | **13:18-19** | The Parable of the Mustard Seed (Kingdom of God compared to a mustard seed) |
| **Luke** | **17:6** | If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can command a tree to be uprooted |
