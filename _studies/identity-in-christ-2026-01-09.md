## Executive Summary

Biblical identity is defined not by self-improvement, but by receiving who God declares a believer to be through Christ. This identity is positional (what God has done) before it is practical (how we live).

## Core Identity Statements

- **New Creation (2 Corinthians 5:17):** "Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come."
- **Children of God (1 John 3:1):** "See what kind of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God; and so we are."
- **Righteousness of God (2 Corinthians 5:21):** "For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God."

## Key Identity Categories

| Category | Description | Key References |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positional | Legal and spiritual standing before God. | Romans 5:1, Ephesians 1:5, 1:13 |
| Relational | Our connection to the Godhead and the Church. | John 15:15, Romans 8:17, 1 Cor 12:27 |
| Functional | Our purpose and role in the world. | 2 Cor 5:20, Matt 5:13-14, 1 Peter 2:9 |

## Positional Identities

- **Justified:** Declared righteous (Romans 5:1).
- **Sanctified:** Set apart as holy (1 Corinthians 1:2).
- **Adopted:** Brought into God's family (Ephesians 1:5).
- **Sealed:** Marked with the Holy Spirit (Ephesians 1:13).

## Relational Identities

- Friends of Christ (John 15:15)
- Co-heirs with Christ (Romans 8:17)
- Bride of Christ (Ephesians 5:25-27)

## Functional Identities

- Ambassadors (2 Corinthians 5:20)
- Royal Priesthood (1 Peter 2:9)
- God's Workmanship (Ephesians 2:10)

## Theological Correlations

- **Linear Progression:** Justification (Legal) → Adoption (Family) → Glorification (Final State).
- **Causal Relationship:** Union with Christ produces character change, which enables effective mission.
- **Psychological Chain:** Identity provides Security, which results in Freedom.

## Transformational Implications

**The Exchange:**

- **Status:** From Guilty to Righteous
- **Relationship:** From Enemies to Children
- **Nature:** From Slaves to Sin to Slaves to Righteousness
- **Destiny:** From Death to Eternal Life

**Practical Application:**

- **Security:** Confidence in salvation (John 10:28-29).
- **Acceptance:** Loved regardless of performance (Romans 8:38-39).
- **Resources:** Access to all spiritual blessings (Ephesians 1:3).
