## Week 1: Gideon's Calling (Judges 6:11-24)

- Discuss Gideon's insecurities and doubts when the angel calls him a "mighty warrior"
- Reflect on times when we feel weak or inadequate for what God is calling us to
- Focus on God's assurance to Gideon: "I will be with you" (v.16)

## Week 2: Gideon's Fleece (Judges 6:36-40)

- Examine Gideon's need for repeated confirmation from God
- Honestly share doubts, fears, need for proof before obeying God
- Discuss how to grow in faith to trust God's will without demanding signs

## Week 3: Gideon's Army Reduced (Judges 7:1-8)

- Note God's winnowing of Gideon's army from 32,000 to 300 men
- Reflect on God's desire to use seemingly inadequate resources
- Surrender personal agendas, strengths, capabilities to experience God's power

## Week 4: Gideon's Unlikely Victory (Judges 7:9-25)

- Recount the unusual battle plan of trumpets and jars
- Discuss examples of God bringing victory in unlikely ways
- Surrender misconceptions about how God works; be open to His ways

## Week 5: Gideon's Lapses (Judges 8:22-28)

- Examine Gideon's incorrect actions after victory (idolatry, polygamy)
- Confess personal pitfalls of not fully surrendering to God
- Commit to continual submission and dependence on the Lord

## Each Week

Open with prayer for humility, conclude with prayer for greater trust/surrender.

Encourage personal examples, transparency about doubts/struggles. Emphasize God's desire to demonstrate His power through willing, humble servants.
