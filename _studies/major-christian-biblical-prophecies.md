Biblical scholars catalog over 1,800 distinct prophecies throughout the Old and New Testaments, accounting for roughly 27% of the biblical text. Because listing every individual prediction is not feasible, the following table details the most significant and widely recognized Christian prophecies, categorized by the First Coming of Christ, major historical events, and eschatological (end-times) expectations.
