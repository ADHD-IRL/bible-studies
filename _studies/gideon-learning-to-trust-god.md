- **Learning to trust God, especially when you feel weak and inadequate:** "But the Lord said to him, 'Surely I will be with you, and you shall defeat Midian as one man.'" (Judges 6:16)
- **Taking unpopular, but necessary stands:** "Then Gideon tore down the altar of Baal that his father had, and cut down the wooden image that was beside it." (Judges 6:25)
- **Finding guidance from God to make difficult decisions about your future:** "The Lord said to Gideon, 'The people are too many for me to give Midian into their hand through them, lest Israel boast over me, saying, 'My own hand has saved me.'" (Judges 7:2)
- **God calls leaders from unlikely settings:** "Now the angel of the Lord came and sat under the terebinth tree at Ophrah, which belonged to Joash the Abiezrite, as his son Gideon was threshing wheat in the winepress to hide it from the Midianites. And the angel of the Lord appeared to him and said, 'Mighty warrior is the Lord with you.'" (Judges 6:11-12)
- **God prefers a few dedicated and disciplined disciples to multitudes of uncommitted workers:** "Then Gideon said to the people, 'Whoever is afraid and trembling may withdraw early tomorrow morning from Mount Gilead.' And twenty-two thousand of the people withdrew, leaving ten thousand." (Judges 7:3)
- **God still uses ordinary things to accomplish His purposes today:** "Then Gideon said to them, 'When you see me and my men crouch at the edge of the camp, do what we do. When I blow the trumpet, I and all who are with me will blow the trumpets, and you blow the trumpets also around the whole camp and shout, 'For the Lord and for Gideon!'" (Judges 7:18)
- **We must guard against forgetting God and failing to teach our children:** "But after the death of Gideon the Israelites prostituted themselves again to the Baals and made Baal-berith their god. And the Israelites did not remember the Lord their God, who had delivered them from the hand of all their enemies on every side, nor did they show any loyalty to the house of Jerubbaal (that is, Gideon) for all the good he had done to Israel." (Judges 8:33-35)
- **God can use ordinary people to do great things:** "...though I am the least in my father's house, and my clan is the weakest in Manasseh." (Judges 6:15)
- **A small number with God can do much:** "With the three hundred men who drank from their hands, Gideon subdued the rest of the army of Midian. The others all fled to the Jordan Valley south of Beth-barah." (Judges 7:25)
- **One can grow from doubt to great faith:** "'If I have found favor in your eyes,' he said, 'let this sign be in my hand: let the fleece on the threshing floor alone be wet with dew, while all the ground be dry.' And God did so that night. He wrung out the fleece, and squeezed a bowlful of water from it." (Judges 6:36-38)
- **Leaders should be courageous and examples for others to follow:** "... Do not be afraid of them, for the Lord your God is with you." (Judges 6:16)
