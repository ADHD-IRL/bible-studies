## 1. The Core Objective

The primary goal of communication is not just the delivery of facts, but the spiritual maturity of the community.

- **Grow into Christ:** Speaking the truth in love allows us to grow in every way into Christ, who is the head.
- **The Triple Filter:** Ensure your words are driven by a **pure heart**, a **good conscience**, and a **sincere faith**.

## 2. The Character of the Speaker

Truth is not merely about the words said, but the transformation of the person saying them.

- **The Old Self:** You are commanded to stop lying to one another because you have "put off the old self" and its former habits.
- **Faithfulness:** God delights in those who act faithfully, whereas "lying lips" are considered an abomination.
- **Rejoicing in Good:** Your heart should not find joy in wrongdoing but should actively "rejoice with the truth".

## 3. Principles for Honest Communication

To share truth effectively, follow these scriptural standards for daily interaction:

- **Directness:** Speak the truth clearly to one another without deceit.
- **Promote Peace:** When making judgments or resolving conflicts, aim for "judgments that are true and make for peace".
- **Reliability:** Recognize that the sum of God's word is truth; therefore, your own words should reflect that same enduring righteousness.

## 4. The Impact of Truth

Understanding why we share the truth helps maintain the motivation to do so, even when it is difficult.

- **Liberation:** Knowing and sharing the truth is the pathway to being "set free".
- **Eternal Standards:** Truth is not temporary; righteous rules and truthful principles are intended to endure forever.
