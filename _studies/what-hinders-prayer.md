A simple study outline you can use for personal Bible study on what hinders prayer, with reflection questions for each section.

## 1) Unconfessed Sin

**Read:** Psalm 66:18, Isaiah 59:1-2, 1 John 1:9. These verses show that sin, especially unconfessed sin, can interfere with fellowship with God and with prayer.

**Reflection questions:**

- Is there any known sin I have been ignoring?
- Have I honestly confessed it to God?
- What step of obedience do I need to take today?

## 2) Wrong Motives

**Read:** James 4:3. This verse warns that prayer can be hindered when requests are driven by selfish desire rather than God's purposes.

**Reflection questions:**

- What am I really asking for, and why?
- Am I seeking God's glory or my own comfort?
- How can I align this request with God's will?

## 3) Unforgiveness

**Read:** Mark 11:25, Matthew 6:14-15. These passages connect prayer with forgiving others, showing that bitterness can block a healthy prayer life.

**Reflection questions:**

- Is there anyone I am refusing to forgive?
- What resentments am I still carrying?
- What would it look like to release that person to God?

## 4) Doubt and Weak Faith

**Read:** Hebrews 11:6, James 1:6-7. These verses teach that God is pleased by faith, and that divided or unstable trust weakens prayer.

**Reflection questions:**

- Do I believe God hears me?
- Where am I struggling to trust Him?
- What promise of God do I need to hold onto?

## 5) Disobedience and Lack of Abiding

**Read:** John 15:7, Proverbs 28:9. These verses show that staying close to God and listening to His word are tied to effective prayer.

**Reflection questions:**

- Am I actually obeying what God has already shown me?
- What does it mean for me to "abide" in Christ this week?
- What habit would help me stay more connected to God?

## 6) Broken Relationships

**Read:** 1 Peter 3:7. This verse specifically warns that strained marital relationships can hinder prayers, showing that how we treat others affects our walk with God.

**Reflection questions:**

- Are there relational tensions I need to address?
- Have I been respectful, patient, and kind in my closest relationships?
- What practical step can I take toward peace?

## Simple Study Method

Use this pattern for each passage:

- Read the verse slowly.
- Write one sentence on what it teaches.
- Answer the reflection question honestly.
- End with a short prayer based on that verse.

## Closing Prayer Prompt

"Lord, show me anything in my life that is hindering my prayers. Give me repentance, faith, forgiveness, and a willing heart to obey You."
