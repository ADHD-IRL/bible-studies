I sat in lukewarm churches for years. I know exactly what I'm talking about.

Holiness has become a forbidden word in the pulpit. We've raised a generation that wants God's blessings without obedience, His promises without repentance, and salvation without change.

## Where the Blame Falls

And the blame falls on the pulpit.

Pastors who look like the world, talk like the world, and cuss like the world. Pastors who roll into the house of God like they just rolled out of bed, who binge the same filth the world watches, some of it outright demonic, then stand up on Sunday and claim to speak for a HOLY God.

This isn't about the price of a suit. It's about reverence. When did modesty, dignity, and honoring God become something the Church is embarrassed about?

## Called to Be Separate, Not Relatable

They call it being "relatable." Turn on almost any nondenominational service and you'll see it.

**THE CHURCH WAS NEVER CALLED TO BE RELATABLE TO THE WORLD. IT WAS CALLED TO BE SEPARATE FROM IT.**

> "But as he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation; Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy."
>
> 1 Peter 1:15-16

Not holy when it's convenient. Not holy on Sunday only. **ALL** manner of conversation: your mouth, your eyes, your clothes, your phone, and what you do when nobody's watching.

> "Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord."
>
> Hebrews 12:14

Read it again: **WITHOUT HOLINESS, NO MAN SHALL SEE THE LORD.**

When's the last time your pastor preached that?

## Trading Conviction for Entertainment

Instead, we get attendance numbers, engagement, fog machines, light shows, and motivational speeches, anything except confronting sin.

We traded conviction for entertainment, reverence for casual, and preaching for a TED talk. Now the pews are full of people who know how to attend church, but have never been taught how to live separated and Spirit-filled.

Paul saw it coming:

> "For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; but after their own lusts shall they heap to themselves teachers, having itching ears."
>
> 2 Timothy 4:3

**IT'S HERE. EVERY SUNDAY.**

## A Charge to Pastors

Pastors, you are not a celebrity. You are not an influencer, a comedian, or anyone's buddy. You're a shepherd. Preach the Word. Confront sin. Live holy in front of your people.

> "My brethren, be not many masters, knowing that we shall receive the greater condemnation."
>
> James 3:1

That should terrify every man who steps behind a pulpit.

## Grace Is Not a License to Sin

And hear me: **GRACE IS NOT A LICENSE TO SIN.** I don't care how many churches tell you it is.

Grace teaches us:

> "...that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world."
>
> Titus 2:12

Real grace doesn't excuse the old life. It drags you out of it.

> "Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord."
>
> 2 Corinthians 6:17

The Church should **LOOK** different, **SOUND** different, and **LIVE** different. If the Holy Ghost lives in you, it should show.

## What the Church Truly Needs

We need preachers who fear God more than they fear losing members, money, or likes, preachers who will preach repentance, holiness, modesty, and separation without apologizing for any of it. And we need believers who stop shopping for a church that makes them comfortable in their sin.

**A CHURCH THAT WON'T PREACH HOLINESS IS FAILING THE PEOPLE IT CLAIMS TO LOVE.**

Jesus didn't bleed so you could stay chained to the same sin He came to break. The Church doesn't need another rebrand, another light show, or another celebrity pastor.

**IT NEEDS REPENTANCE. IT NEEDS THE HOLY GHOST. IT NEEDS THE FEAR OF GOD. IT NEEDS HOLINESS.**

## Conclusion: A Call to Action

I'll tell you where I am now. After years in lukewarm churches, God led me to a Holy Ghost-filled church with a pastor who walks what he preaches. It changed everything.

If your church never calls you out of sin, get out. Your soul is worth more than your comfort.

> "Be ye holy; for I am holy."
>
> 1 Peter 1:16

Does your pastor still preach holiness? Yes or no.
