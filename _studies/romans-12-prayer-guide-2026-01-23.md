The 7-Day Romans 12 "Life-Transformation" Guide

## Day 1: The Integrity of Love (v. 9)

- **Focus:** *Anypókritos* (Unmasked Love).
- **Prayer:** "Lord, remove the masks I wear to impress others. Grant me a visceral hatred for what is evil and a 'glued-on' commitment to what is good."
- **Action:** Identify one area where you've been "performing" kindness. Perform a secret act of service today where you receive zero credit.

## Day 2: The Family Connection (v. 10)

- **Focus:** *Philadelphia* (Brotherly Affection) and the Honor Race.
- **Prayer:** "Father, thank You for adopting me. Help me to see my fellow believers as true brothers and sisters. Give me the humility to 'win' the race of honoring others today."
- **Action:** Send a text or handwritten note to someone in your community, specifically highlighting a character trait you admire in them.

## Day 3: The Spiritual Fire (v. 11)

- **Focus:** *Zeontes* (Boiling Spirit).
- **Prayer:** "Holy Spirit, fuel my inner fire. Forgive my spiritual sloth and my tendency to go through the motions. Reignite my passion for serving You."
- **Action:** Spend 10 minutes in "fervent" prayer or worship before starting your work. Work on your most mundane task today as if you were doing it directly for Jesus.

## Day 4: The Survival Triad (v. 12)

- **Focus:** Hope, Patience, and Constant Prayer.
- **Prayer:** "Lord, when the pressure (*thlipsis*) increases, keep my eyes on the hope of Your return. Help me stay under the weight without complaining, and keep my line of communication with You open at all times."
- **Action:** Every time you check your phone today, use that as a "trigger" to offer a 15-second prayer for someone in need.

## Day 5: Radical Outreach (v. 13)

- **Focus:** *Philoxenia* (Love of Strangers).
- **Prayer:** "Lord, Your generosity knows no bounds. Help me to be a conduit of Your provision. Give me eyes to see the 'stranger' and a heart to pursue them with hospitality."
- **Action:** Contribute to a need you've been ignoring (financial or practical) or invite someone you don't know well to coffee or a meal.

## Day 6: The Empathy Shift (v. 14-16)

- **Focus:** Blessing and Harmony.
- **Prayer:** "Jesus, You blessed those who mocked You. Help me to rejoice in others' successes without jealousy and to sit in the ashes with those who are grieving."
- **Action:** Identify someone who has been a "critic" in your life and pray a specific blessing over their family and health today.

## Day 7: The Victory of Good (v. 17-21)

- **Focus:** Overcoming Evil.
- **Prayer:** "Father, I relinquish my right to get even. I trust Your justice. Give me the strength to 'heap burning coals' of kindness on those who wrong me today."
- **Action:** If you are in a conflict, take the first step toward peace (v. 18), even if you aren't the one at fault.

## Summary of the Path
