## Report Insight: The "Renewed Mind" Connection

The statistical correlation between **Romans 12:2** and **Ephesians 4:23** is particularly strong because they are the only two passages in the Pauline epistles that explicitly link the "renewal" (*anakainosis*) of the "mind" (*nous*) to the rejection of worldly patterns. This suggests a consistent pedagogical framework used by Paul when instructing new Christian communities.
