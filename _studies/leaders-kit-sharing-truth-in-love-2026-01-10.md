This Bible Study Leader's Kit is designed to help you lead a group through the practical application of the scriptures regarding honesty, love, and community growth.

**Theme:** Equipping the Body for Sincere Fellowship

## Part 1: The Handbook (Theology & Framework)

This section outlines the scriptural foundations for how we communicate.

- **The Ultimate Goal:** We speak the truth in love to ensure the body grows up in every way into Christ, who is our head.
- **The Heart of the Matter:** Our goal is a love that comes from a pure heart, a good conscience, and a sincere faith.
- **The Standard of Honesty:** We are to put off the "old self" and its practices, which includes a commitment to not lie to one another.
- **God's View of Truth:** Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord, but He delights in those who act with faithfulness.
- **The Power of Truth:** Knowing the truth is the specific path to spiritual freedom.

## Part 2: The Code of Conduct (Group Standards)

Use this as a covenant for your Bible study or small group.

1. **Direct Communication:** We will speak the truth to one another directly.
2. **Peaceful Resolution:** Our judgments and discussions will be aimed at making peace within our community.
3. **Joyful Integrity:** We will not rejoice in wrongdoing or gossip, but we will rejoice when the truth is revealed and honored.
4. **Upholding the Word:** We acknowledge that the sum of God's word is truth and that His rules for our conduct endure forever.

## Part 3: Conflict Resolution Worksheet

A step-by-step guide for resolving disagreements biblically.

| Phase | Action Step | Scriptural Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Check for a pure heart and sincere faith before speaking. | 1 Timothy 1:5 |
| Engagement | Speak the truth clearly but wrap it in love for the sake of growth. | Ephesians 4:15 |
| Correction | Identify and "put off" any old habits of deceit or lying. | Colossians 3:9 |
| Resolution | Seek a judgment that is true and leads to lasting peace. | Zechariah 8:16 |

## Part 4: Scripture Reference Sheet

Keep these verses accessible for your members during discussions.

- **Ephesians 4:15:** Speaking truth in love to grow into Christ.
- **1 Timothy 1:5:** Aim for love from a pure heart and sincere faith.
- **Proverbs 12:22:** Lying is an abomination; faithfulness is God's delight.
- **John 8:32:** The truth sets you free.
- **Colossians 3:9:** Do not lie; put off the old self.
- **Zechariah 8:16:** Speak truth and make judgments for peace.
- **1 Corinthians 13:6:** Love rejoices with the truth.
- **Psalm 119:160:** The sum of God's word is truth.
