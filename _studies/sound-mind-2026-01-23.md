## Primary "Sound Mind" Reference

> "For God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control."
> 2 Timothy 1:7

- **Greek Origin:** The term "self-control" (*sophronismos*) literally translates to **"sound mind"** or "self-discipline."
- **Direct Link:** This is the most explicit biblical reference to mental clarity and stability.
- **Context:** It is presented as a **gift from God**, rather than a state achieved through human willpower alone.

## Key Biblical Themes Related to Mental Health

### 1. Peace of Mind

- **Philippians 4:6-7:** Focuses on replacing anxiety with prayer and thanksgiving to receive a peace that "surpasses all understanding."
- **John 14:27:** Jesus distinguishes His peace from the world's temporary peace.
- **Isaiah 26:3:** Promises "perfect peace" for those whose minds are stayed on God.

### 2. Mind Renewal and Transformation

- **Romans 12:2:** Directs believers to be transformed by the **renewal of the mind** rather than conforming to external pressures.
- **Ephesians 4:23:** Highlights the spiritual nature of mental renewal.
- **Colossians 3:2:** Encourages "setting the mind" on higher perspectives to manage earthly stressors.

### 3. Freedom from Anxiety

- **Matthew 6:25-34:** A foundational teaching on worry, mentioned six times in this specific passage.
- **1 Peter 5:7:** The invitation to "cast" anxieties onto God because of His personal care.
- **Psalm 94:19:** Acknowledges that when internal "cares" are many, divine consolation brings cheer.

### 4. Self-Control (Fruit of the Spirit)

- **Galatians 5:22-23:** Self-control is the culmination of the Spirit's work in a person, directly supporting mental discipline.

## Correlations & Connections

A Sound Mind is Connected to:

1. **Spirit of Power:** Mental strength derived from spiritual empowerment.
2. **Love:** Healthy social and spiritual relationships as a support pillar.
3. **Prayer:** A practical discipline that correlates with internal peace.
4. **Truth:** Mental freedom achieved by aligning thoughts with reality.
5. **Hope:** A "God of hope" providing joy and peace as fuel for the mind.

## Mental Struggles in Scripture

The Bible does not shy away from the reality of mental and emotional distress:

- **David's Distress:** Raw emotional transparency in **Psalms 42, 43, & 55**.
- **Elijah's Depression:** Burnout and suicidal ideation in **1 Kings 19**.
- **Job's Anguish:** The psychological process of suffering and grief.
- **Jesus' Anguish:** Sorrow described as "even to death" in **Matthew 26:38**.

### The Philippians 4:8 "Filter"

Paul provides a checklist for healthy thinking:

- **True:** Align with reality.
- **Honorable:** Worthy of respect.
- **Just:** Righteous and fair.
- **Pure:** Morally clean.
- **Lovely:** Beautiful and attractive.
- **Commendable:** Praiseworthy.
- **Excellent:** Of high quality.

## Practical Biblical Wisdom

The Bible presents mental health as a **holistic** experience involving the spirit, soul, and body. It is **relational** (requiring God and community), **transformational** (requiring ongoing work), and **honest** (validating the human experience of suffering while providing resources for healing).
