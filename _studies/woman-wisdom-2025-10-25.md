The Bible warns about several types of women who can lead others, particularly men, away from godly wisdom, integrity, and moral purity. These warnings, found mainly in Proverbs and throughout Scripture, describe character traits and behaviors that cause spiritual and relational harm rather than goodness and faithfulness.

## The Types of Women the Bible Warns About

1. **The Adulterous or Immoral Woman**
   Proverbs repeatedly warns against the *immoral woman*, one who uses flattery, seduction, and beauty to entice men to sin. Her lips are said to "drip honey," but her path "leads to death" (Proverbs 5:3-5). This woman symbolizes temptation and unfaithfulness that destroy integrity and families.

2. **The Foolish Woman**
   A "foolish woman" tears down her house with her own hands (Proverbs 14:1). She lacks wisdom, discretion, and humility, acting impulsively and destructively. Her choices bring instability to those around her.

3. **The Contentious or Quarrelsome Woman**
   Proverbs teaches that living with such a woman is worse than living in a desert (Proverbs 21:19). She is argumentative, nagging, and unable to bring peace within her home. Her behavior drains emotional energy and harmony.

4. **The Seductive or Harlotrous Woman**
   This type of woman is described as aggressive, flirtatious, and immodest, "with the attire of a harlot and a crafty heart" (Proverbs 7:10). Her charm masks spiritual emptiness and leads others into sin and regret.

5. **The Lovely but Indiscreet Woman**
   Proverbs 11:22 compares "a beautiful woman who shows no discretion" to a gold ring in a pig's snout. The message warns that physical beauty without moral or spiritual wisdom is meaningless and even dangerous.

6. **The Gossiping or Idle Woman**
   In 1 Timothy 5:13, Paul warns that idle women may go "about from house to house, gossiping and meddling." Such behavior shows a lack of discipline and can destroy reputations and relationships.

7. **The Rebellious or Proud Woman**
   Scripture also warns against women (and people generally) who are unsubmissive to godly order, self-centered, or driven by vanity and pride. Figures like Jezebel and Delilah are highlighted as examples of manipulative rebellion that leads others into sin and destruction.

## The Contrast: The Godly Woman

In contrast, Proverbs 31 presents an ideal woman who is wise, industrious, honor-driven, kind, and faithful. She "does her husband good and not harm all the days of her life" (Proverbs 31:12) and represents discretion, humility, and spiritual strength.

In short, the Bible warns primarily against immoral, foolish, rebellious, nagging, and seductive women, not because of gender bias, but as a moral teaching for all believers to value wisdom, purity, humility, and faithfulness over charm and deceit.
